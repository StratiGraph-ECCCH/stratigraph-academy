# Where the rest lives

A short map of what this manual deliberately does not contain, and where to
find it instead.

**The Extended Matrix method** — what a stratigraphic unit is, how a
reconstruction is argued, what a virtual unit means: EM Academy, and the
published method.

**EMStudio** (the sovereign Extended Matrix editor) and **EM Tools** (the
Blender add-on): their own manuals, in their own repositories.

**s3Dgraphy** — the library that holds the graph and the ontology every tool
shares: its own documentation on Read the Docs.

**stratigraph-templates** — the recording sheets and the controlled
vocabularies: its own documentation, in English and Italian.

**Building a client, or installing a node** — the REST doors of a room, the
node's own administration API, the vocabularies, deployment and OpenShift: the
`docs/` of `stratigraph-server`, published with the code it describes.

**Courses** — structured learning paths over the whole toolset: the StratiGraph
Academy.
